# Serenity

A complete indie game background music composition 
representing a full day's emotional journey.

## Structure

### Sunrise (Measures 1-8)
Hopeful awakening as the sun rises
- A minor → F Major → C Major → G Major
- Dynamics: mf building to f
- Piano flows with eighth notes
- Organ whisper-soft foundation (ppp)

### Midday/Plateau (Measures 9-20)
Sustained power and energy (loopable for gameplay)
- F Major → C Major → G Major (repeated)
- Steady confident rhythm
- Perfect for ambient gameplay loop

### Sunset (Measures 17-24)
Transition from light to darkness
- Piano increases complexity (sixteenth notes)
- Shift towards minor tonality
- Harp becomes more active

### Night (Measures 21-31)
Dark but epic resolution
- C minor → B♭ Major → A♭ Major (the dark epic progression!)
- pp dynamics (very soft)
- Piano sparse, fading
- Organ sustained whole notes
- Final resolution → silence
- Complete 24-hour journey ends peacefully

## Instrumentation

**Piano**: Melodic lead, carries emotional arc
**Church Organ**: Foundation, sustain, spiritual depth
**Harp**: Delicate texture, atmosphere

## Technical Details

- **Time Signature**: 4/4
- **Tempo**: 120BPM
- **Duration**: 1 Minute
- **Key**: G Major (day) → C Minor (night, key modulation)
- **Dynamic Range**: mf → f → pp → ppp (complete arc)

## Composition Journey

Created entirely by ear using Crescendo notation software.
No formal music theory training—pure instinct, feeling, and listening.

## Inspiration

This piece captures the emotional journey of a complete day:
- Sunrise = Hope, awakening, growth
- Midday = Power, sustained energy, confidence
- Sunset = Transition, mystery, change
- Night = Darkness, peace, resolution, sleep

Perfect for indie games, meditation, or atmospheric gaming moments.
